# Jharkhand JobAlert X

Jharkhand's #1 free job portal for JPSC, JSSC, Rojgar Mela, Police, Teacher Vacancies, and Sarkari Naukri 2026.

## What We Offer

Jharkhand JobAlert X provides verified job alerts, recruitment notifications, and career resources for Jharkhand government and private sector jobs across all 24 districts.

### Job Categories Covered

- **JPSC Recruitment** - Civil Services, CDPO, Engineering & Allied Posts
- **JSSC Recruitment** - CGL, Excise Constable & Teacher Exams
- **Rojgar Mela** - District-wise jobs fairs and placement drives
- **Private Jobs** - Tata Steel, Jindal, Adani & Corporate openings
- **Central Jobs** - SSC, Railway, Banking, UPSC & Defence
- **Other Jharkhand Jobs** - High Court, Police, Health & Government Depts
